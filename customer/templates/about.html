About Us

Revolutionizing Automobile Services

FixiGo is a comprehensive automobile workshop management system designed to bridge the gap between vehicle owners and service providers. Our platform simplifies the entire process from booking to billing.

With a focus on transparency, efficiency, and customer satisfaction, we've helped thousands of customers get their vehicles serviced by trusted mechanics across India.
